Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026. Last Updated: October 19, 2026.

Dr. Alka Chadha and her executive management office (operating from Hope & Care Centre, Navi Mumbai, India) are dedicated to preserving the privacy and data rights of conference organizers, corporate clients, event planners, and website visitors.

This Privacy Policy sets forth our operational practices in rigorous alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 and the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Identity of Data Fiduciary

Under the DPDP Act 2023, the designated Data Fiduciary for this portal is:

Executive Office: Dr. Alka Chadha
Operating Entity: Hope & Care Centre
Registered Address: Plot No. 41/1, Sector 30A, Vashi, Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra 400703, India
Contact Email: privacy@shinewithalka.com / speaking@shinewithalka.com

2. Principles of Data Minimization & Specified Purpose

We strictly collect only the personal information required to execute our professional speaking services. We do not gather extraneous profiling indicators. When you submit a keynote inquiry via our booking console, we collect:

  • Your full name and professional email address.
  • Your organization, corporate body, or host association name.
  • Event details including proposed date, event city and country, format, and audience size.
  • Any specific message or presentation objectives you volunteer.

Specified Purpose: This data is processed exclusively to assess date availability, prepare keynote fee proposals, coordinate pre-speech logistics, and facilitate necessary contracts. It is never sold, leased, rented, or repurposed for unrelated marketing campaigns.

3. Lawful Basis & Notice Under DPDP Act 2023

We process your personal data on the lawful basis of informed, explicit consent provided at the moment of submission through our booking form. You possess the absolute right to withdraw consent at any time without retroactive penalty.

4. Rights of Data Principals

Under Chapter III of the DPDP Act 2023 and Chapter III of GDPR, you possess the following actionable rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You may demand correction of inaccurate personal data or complete erasure of your contact details once speaking logistics are concluded.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You may register an inquiry or grievance with our designated Grievance Officer.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate an individual to exercise your data rights in the event of death or incapacity.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes local storage strictly for functional preferences: preserving your selection between Dark Obsidian Mode and Light Alabaster Mode, and recording your cookie consent status. We do not deploy third-party advertising pixels, cross-site trackers, or behavioral surveillance cookies. Review our Cookie Policy for detailed breakdowns.

6. Security Measures

We deploy strict physical and technical safeguards to protect your personal data from accidental loss, unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All web traffic is encrypted using modern TLS (HTTPS) protocols.

7. Grievance Officer & Redressal Protocol

In conformity with Section 13(1) of the DPDP Act 2023, Dr. Alka Chadha has designated an internal Grievance Officer for handling privacy inquiries:

Grievance Officer: Legal & Privacy Desk
Address: Hope & Care Centre, Plot No. 41/1, Sector 30A, Vashi, Navi Mumbai, MH 400703, India
Email: privacy@shinewithalka.com
Resolution Timeline: Grievances will be acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within 30 days.
