Official Introduction Bios

Short Program Bio (50 Words)

Standard Introduction Bio (150 Words)

Dr. Alka Chadha is a medical physician, author, and international keynote speaker with more than thirty years of clinical experience. As the founder and director of Hope & Care Centre in Navi Mumbai, she has spent decades helping individuals, couples, and leaders understand how suppressed emotional strain translates into physical exhaustion and organizational friction. Serving as President of the Professional Speakers Association of India (PSAI) Mumbai Chapter (2025-26), Dr. Alka is known for her signature philosophy, "We are rivers, not rocks." She has addressed international audiences at the German Speakers Association (GSA) Convention, corporate forums across India, and assemblies in Australia and Singapore. Dr. Alka is the author of The Magical Couples and Shukriya Zindagi, alongside her widely read executive monograph Care About Brain. She provides actionable, evidence-based tools that transform workplace conflict into collaborative momentum.

Technical Specifications

Audio / Visual & Staging Rider

To guarantee the highest quality experience for your attendees, please review Dr. Alka's technical staging preferences:

Microphone

Wireless hands-free microphone preferred (Countryman or DPA headset, or secure wireless lavalier). Dr. Alka moves actively across the stage and interacts with attendees.

Visual Projection

16:9 widescreen presentation display via HDMI. Slides are clean, visual, and low-text. Confidence monitor on the floor facing the stage is appreciated for plenary halls.

Stage Layout

Open stage or clear speaking area. No podium or fixed lectern is required; Dr. Alka speaks directly to the audience without physical barriers.

Recording & Video

Video recording of keynotes is permitted with advance written notice. A digital copy of full-length footage is requested for speaker archives.
