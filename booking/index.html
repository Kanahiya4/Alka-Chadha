Frequently Asked Questions for Conference Chairs

How far in advance should we secure dates?

For international summits and national conventions, booking 2 to 6 months in advance is recommended. Regional executive retreats and corporate workshops can typically be accommodated with 4 to 8 weeks lead time depending on calendar availability.

Can keynotes be customized for our industry?

Yes. Every engagement includes a structured 45-minute alignment briefing with your committee to weave your sector's unique stress vectors, organizational terminology, and strategic goals directly into Dr. Alka's keynote delivery.

Are virtual and hybrid keynotes supported?

Yes. Dr. Alka delivers live broadcast-grade virtual keynotes and interactive webinars equipped with multi-camera anatomical demonstrations and real-time audience engagement tools.

What languages does Dr. Alka deliver in?

Keynotes and masterclasses are delivered fluently in English, Hindi, or a natural bilingual blend, tailored to the delegate profile of your convention or corporate gathering.
