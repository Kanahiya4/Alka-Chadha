Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026.

Dr. Alka Chadha's portal believes in ethical web architecture. We respect your attention and your right to privacy. This policy provides complete disclosure regarding how this website utilizes browser cookies and local storage tokens.

1. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies and browser local storage are compact data strings stored securely on your local device to enable website functionality and retain essential user preferences between browsing sessions.

2. Categories of Storage We Deploy

Strictly Necessary Functional Storage:

Token: alka_theme_preference
Purpose: Retains your visual theme selection (Dark Obsidian Mode or Light Alabaster Mode) so your eyes are not strained across page transitions. Contains no personal data.

Token: alka_cookie_consent_v1
Purpose: Remembers whether you have acknowledged our privacy banner, avoiding repetitive prompts on every page visit.

Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers:

We do not deploy Meta Pixel, Google Remarketing, TikTok pixels, or cross-site tracking beacons. Your browsing history on this portal is neither logged nor monetized.
