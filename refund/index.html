Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026.

This policy outlines the financial and cancellation protocols governing keynote speaking agreements, corporate executive workshops, and literary purchases coordinated with Dr. Alka Chadha's speaking office.

1. Keynote Speaking Retainers & Calendar Commitments

To secure an exclusive event date on Dr. Alka Chadha's calendar, a standard 50% non-refundable retainer deposit is required alongside the signed Speaking Agreement. Because event dates are held exclusively and conflicting corporate invitations for that calendar window are declined, the following cancellation terms apply:

  • Host Cancellation More Than 60 Days Prior: The 50% retainer deposit is retained, but 100% of the deposit may be credited toward a rescheduled engagement within 12 months, subject to calendar alignment.
  • Host Cancellation Between 30 and 60 Days Prior: 50% of the total contract fee is retained. Rescheduling credit is subject to executive discretion and travel feasibility.
  • Host Cancellation Less Than 30 Days Prior: 100% of the total agreed speaking fee remains due and payable, as alternative bookings cannot be scheduled and extensive keynote preparation has been completed.

2. Speaker Inability & Force Majeure

In the improbable event that Dr. Alka Chadha is prevented from fulfilling a keynote engagement due to documented acute medical emergency, severe weather disruption, or act of state (Force Majeure):

  • Dr. Alka's office will promptly coordinate a virtual broadcast option if technically feasible.
  • If virtual delivery is unworkable, all fees and deposits received from the client for that engagement will be refunded within 14 business days, or applied to an agreed replacement date without financial penalty.

3. Corporate Masterclasses & Workshops

Customized multi-day or half-day corporate masterclasses require significant advance research and tailoring. Cancellations follow the identical notice tiers outlined in Section 1. Any non-refundable travel expenses already incurred (such as non-flexible airfares or visa clearances) remain the responsibility of the host organization.

4. Books & Literary Publications

For individual retail purchases of Shukriya Zindagi or The Magical Couples completed via third-party vendors (such as Amazon, Notion Press, or regional bookstores), return and refund protocols are governed by the respective retailer's purchase terms.

For bulk book orders coordinated directly with our office for summit attendees: cancellations are accepted with a full refund up until the point at which bulk printing or shipment is initiated.
