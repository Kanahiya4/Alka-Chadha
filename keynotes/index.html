Keynote 01: Global Leadership

We Are Rivers, Not Rocks

How Egoless Speakers & Leaders Build Unstoppable Influence.

Rocks resist the current, creating friction, noise, and eventual erosion. Rivers yield to obstacles, carve through solid granite over time, and nourish entire continents along their path. In modern leadership, rigid defensiveness breaks teams. Fluid humility conquers markets.

Audience Takeaways:

  • Dismantling the defensive ego triggers that paralyze executive decisions.
  • The neurobiology of authentic presence: why teams naturally rally around humble conviction.
  • Practical conversational strategies to defuse boardroom polarization in real time.
Ideal Audience: Global Conventions, Executive Summits, Senior Leadership Retreats.
Standard Formats: 45-60 Minute Keynote or 3-Hour Interactive Masterclass.

Keynote 02: Mind-Body Health

Your Issues Get into Your Tissues

The Biology of Stress, Fatigue, and Sustainable Resilience.

Every unaddressed workplace conflict, chronic anxiety spike, and suppressed frustration creates a measurable hormonal cascade. High-performing leaders often ignore early physical signals until severe burnout or metabolic fatigue forces a halt.

Audience Takeaways:

  • The cellular link: how chronic cortisol and sympathetic hyper-arousal impair executive cognition.
  • The ninety-second autonomic composure reset protocol for high-stress corporate negotiations.
  • Architecting organizational environments that nurture vitality instead of chronic depletion.
Ideal Audience: Corporate Leadership, HR & People Summits, Healthcare Conferences.
Standard Formats: 45-75 Minute Keynote or Half-Day Executive Workshop.

Professional Event Standards

Audio-Visual & Stage Technical Rider

To ensure an impactful and flawless keynote experience for your audience, please review Dr. Alka Chadha's standard presentation requirements.

1. Audio Specifications

Wireless DPA or Countryman omnidirectional headset microphone preferred. Dr. Alka demonstrates physical models and moves freely across the stage. Handheld microphones or fixed podium microphones are not recommended.

2. Visual & Display Standards

Standard 16:9 high-definition screen. Slides are visual-first with minimal text. Wireless presentation clicker with confidence monitor facing downstage.

3. Stage Floor & Environment

Clear, unobstructed stage area. If a stationary podium is present, please position it off-center. A small table downstage right for interactive props and water is appreciated.

4. Recording & Intellectual Property

Organizers may record the keynote for internal non-commercial archival distribution. Commercial resale, television broadcast, or public streaming requires prior written authorization under the Speaking Agreement.
